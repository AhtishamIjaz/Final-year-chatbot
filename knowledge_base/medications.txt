MEDICATION REFERENCE GUIDE
Source: British National Formulary (BNF), WHO Model Formulary, Pakistan Drug Regulatory Authority

════════════════════════════════════════
ANALGESICS (PAIN RELIEF)
════════════════════════════════════════

PARACETAMOL (Acetaminophen) — Brand: Panadol, Calpol
- Class: Non-opioid analgesic and antipyretic
- Uses: Mild to moderate pain, fever
- Adult dose: 500–1000mg every 4–6 hours; MAX 4g/day (4000mg)
- Child dose: 10–15 mg/kg every 4–6 hours
- Side effects: Generally well tolerated; OVERDOSE causes acute liver failure (antidote: N-acetylcysteine)
- Contraindications: Severe liver disease
- Special note: Safest OTC pain reliever in pregnancy, for elderly, and with kidney disease

IBUPROFEN — Brand: Brufen, Nurofen, Advil
- Class: Non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drug (NSAID)
- Uses: Pain (especially inflammatory), fever, menstrual cramps, arthritis
- Adult dose: 200–400mg every 6–8 hours; MAX 1200mg/day OTC (up to 2400mg/day prescribed)
- Side effects: GI irritation/ulceration (take with food), fluid retention, kidney impairment, increased BP, cardiovascular risk
- Contraindications: Peptic ulcer disease, severe kidney/heart/liver disease, pregnancy (especially third trimester), aspirin allergy
- Interactions: Warfarin (increased bleeding risk), ACE inhibitors (reduced efficacy + kidney risk), lithium

DICLOFENAC — Brand: Voltaren, Cataflam
- Class: NSAID
- Uses: Arthritis, musculoskeletal pain, dental pain, gout
- Dose: 50mg 2–3 times daily or 75mg SR twice daily; MAX 150mg/day
- Same precautions as ibuprofen; higher cardiovascular risk at high doses

ASPIRIN — Brand: Aspro, Disprin
- Analgesic dose: 300–600mg every 4–6 hours; MAX 4g/day
- Anti-platelet dose: 75–100mg once daily (for stroke/MI prevention)
- NEVER give to children under 16 (risk of Reye's syndrome)
- Contraindications: Peptic ulcer, bleeding disorders, third trimester pregnancy
- Drug interactions: NSAIDs, anticoagulants, methotrexate

TRAMADOL — Brand: Tramal, Ultram
- Class: Weak opioid agonist + serotonin/norepinephrine reuptake inhibitor
- Uses: Moderate to severe pain
- Dose: 50–100mg every 4–6 hours; MAX 400mg/day
- Side effects: Nausea, dizziness, constipation, drowsiness, SEIZURE RISK (especially with SSRIs), dependence potential
- Controlled substance — requires prescription

════════════════════════════════════════
ANTIBIOTICS
════════════════════════════════════════
IMPORTANT: Antibiotics only work against bacteria. Do NOT use for viral infections (cold, flu). Always complete the full course.

AMOXICILLIN — Brand: Amoxil, Flemoxin
- Class: Penicillin antibiotic (beta-lactam)
- Uses: Strep throat, otitis media (ear infection), sinusitis, pneumonia, H. pylori (with other agents), UTI
- Dose: 250–500mg every 8 hours or 500mg–1g every 12 hours; 7–10 day course
- Side effects: Diarrhea, nausea, rash (especially if mononucleosis)
- Contraindications: Penicillin allergy (risk of anaphylaxis — use macrolide instead)

AMOXICILLIN-CLAVULANATE (Co-amoxiclav) — Brand: Augmentin
- Amoxicillin + beta-lactamase inhibitor (clavulanate) — broader spectrum
- Uses: RTI, dental, skin infections, UTI, animal bites
- Dose: 625mg every 8 hours or 1g every 12 hours; take with food to reduce GI upset

AZITHROMYCIN — Brand: Zithromax, Azithral
- Class: Macrolide antibiotic
- Uses: Community-acquired pneumonia, atypical organisms (Chlamydia, Mycoplasma, Legionella), STIs, MAC prophylaxis, typhoid (some resistance)
- Dose: 500mg day 1, then 250mg days 2–5 (Z-pack); or 500mg once daily x 3 days
- Side effects: GI upset (nausea, diarrhea), QT prolongation (cardiac risk), hepatotoxicity
- Interactions: Antacids, statins, warfarin

CIPROFLOXACIN — Brand: Cipro, Ciprolet
- Class: Fluoroquinolone antibiotic
- Uses: UTI, prostatitis, typhoid, GI infections (Salmonella, Shigella), respiratory infections
- Dose: 250–750mg every 12 hours
- Side effects: GI upset, photosensitivity, tendinopathy/tendon rupture (especially with steroids), QT prolongation, seizures, CNS effects (insomnia, dizziness)
- Contraindications: Children (risk of cartilage damage), pregnancy
- Interactions: Antacids, dairy (reduced absorption — take 2h apart), theophylline, warfarin

METRONIDAZOLE — Brand: Flagyl
- Class: Nitroimidazole antibiotic
- Uses: Anaerobic infections, Trichomonas vaginalis, bacterial vaginosis, Giardia, Entamoeba histolytica (amoebic dysentery), H. pylori (with PPI + amoxicillin/clarithromycin), C. difficile (oral for mild disease)
- Dose: 400–500mg every 8 hours or 2g single dose (for STIs)
- Side effects: Metallic taste, nausea, dark urine
- CONTRAINDICATION: Alcohol (severe Antabuse reaction — nausea, vomiting, flushing) — avoid alcohol during AND 48h after treatment

TRIMETHOPRIM (or Co-trimoxazole/Bactrim — TMP-SMX)
- Uses: Uncomplicated UTI (trimethoprim), PCP pneumonia prophylaxis/treatment, MRSA skin infections (TMP-SMX)
- Dose: 200mg twice daily for 7 days (UTI)
- Side effects: Rash, nausea, photosensitivity; Rare: Stevens-Johnson syndrome, marrow suppression

DOXYCYCLINE — Brand: Vibramycin, Doxylin
- Class: Tetracycline antibiotic
- Uses: Malaria prophylaxis, Chlamydia, rickettsia, community-acquired pneumonia (atypical), acne, Lyme disease, brucellosis
- Dose: 100mg twice daily
- Side effects: Photosensitivity (sunscreen essential), oesophageal ulceration (take with full glass of water, remain upright 30 min), GI upset
- Contraindications: Pregnancy, children under 12 (permanent tooth discoloration)

════════════════════════════════════════
CARDIOVASCULAR MEDICATIONS
════════════════════════════════════════

AMLODIPINE — Brand: Norvasc, Istin
- Class: Dihydropyridine Calcium Channel Blocker
- Uses: Hypertension, stable angina
- Dose: 5–10mg once daily
- Side effects: Peripheral oedema (ankle swelling), flushing, headache, palpitations
- Note: Safe in asthma; minimal drug interactions compared to other antihypertensives

LISINOPRIL / ENALAPRIL — Brand: Zestril, Vasotec
- Class: ACE Inhibitor
- Uses: Hypertension, heart failure, post-MI, diabetic nephropathy protection
- Dose: Lisinopril 5–40mg once daily
- Side effects: Dry persistent cough (in ~20% of patients — class effect), hyperkalaemia, first-dose hypotension, angioedema (rare but serious — stop immediately and seek emergency care)
- Contraindications: Pregnancy (teratogenic), bilateral renal artery stenosis, history of angioedema
- Monitor: Potassium and creatinine levels

LOSARTAN / VALSARTAN — Brand: Cozaar, Diovan
- Class: Angiotensin Receptor Blocker (ARB)
- Uses: Hypertension, heart failure, diabetic nephropathy — used when ACE inhibitor not tolerated (no cough)
- Dose: Losartan 25–100mg once daily
- Same contraindications as ACE inhibitors except no cough side effect
- Do NOT combine with ACE inhibitor (increased risk of hypotension, hyperkalaemia)

ATENOLOL / METOPROLOL / BISOPROLOL
- Class: Beta-adrenergic Blockers
- Uses: Hypertension, angina, arrhythmia, heart failure (bisoprolol, carvedilol), post-MI, hyperthyroidism, anxiety (propranolol)
- Dose: Atenolol 25–100mg once daily; Bisoprolol 1.25–10mg once daily
- Side effects: Bradycardia, fatigue, cold extremities, impotence, depression, bronchoconstriction
- Contraindications: Asthma/COPD (beta-1 selective somewhat safer but still caution), heart block, severe bradycardia
- Note: Never stop suddenly — taper dose (risk of rebound hypertension/angina)

FUROSEMIDE — Brand: Lasix
- Class: Loop Diuretic
- Uses: Heart failure, oedema, hypertension
- Dose: 20–80mg once or twice daily
- Side effects: Hypokalemia (low potassium — monitor), dehydration, ototoxicity (high IV doses), gout
- Monitor: Electrolytes (potassium), kidney function, blood pressure

ATORVASTATIN / ROSUVASTATIN — Brand: Lipitor, Crestor
- Class: HMG-CoA Reductase Inhibitor (Statin)
- Uses: High cholesterol (LDL reduction), primary and secondary cardiovascular prevention
- Dose: Atorvastatin 10–80mg at night; Rosuvastatin 5–40mg
- Side effects: Myopathy/rhabdomyolysis (muscle pain + weakness + dark urine — STOP and seek review), hepatotoxicity (rare), GI upset, diabetes risk
- Interactions: Grapefruit juice (increases levels — avoid); some antibiotics (macrolides) increase toxicity risk
- Monitor: CK levels if myopathy suspected; LFTs at baseline

NITRATES (GTN / Isosorbide mononitrate)
- Uses: Angina treatment and prevention
- GTN sublingual: 0.5mg under tongue at onset of angina → repeat after 5 min → if 3 doses ineffective CALL 1122 (possible MI)
- Side effects: Headache, flushing, postural hypotension
- Contraindication: Phosphodiesterase-5 inhibitors (sildenafil/tadalafil — severe hypotension)

════════════════════════════════════════
DIABETES MEDICATIONS
════════════════════════════════════════

METFORMIN — Brand: Glucophage
- Class: Biguanide — first-line for Type 2 DM
- Mechanism: Reduces hepatic glucose production; improves insulin sensitivity
- Dose: Start 500mg once or twice daily with meals; increase to 2000–2550mg/day
- Side effects: GI (nausea, diarrhea, metallic taste) — minimized by taking with food and slow titration; Lactic acidosis (rare, serious — avoid in renal impairment eGFR <30)
- Benefits: No hypoglycemia, weight neutral or modest loss, cardiovascular benefit
- Hold before contrast media administration

GLIBENCLAMIDE / GLIMEPIRIDE (Sulfonylureas)
- Uses: Type 2 DM — stimulate insulin release
- Side effects: Hypoglycemia (most significant risk), weight gain
- Contraindications: Type 1 DM, renal/hepatic impairment, pregnancy

INSULIN TYPES:
- Rapid-acting (NovoRapid, Humalog): Onset 10–20 min, peak 1–3h, duration 3–5h — give just before meals
- Short-acting (Actrapid, Humulin R): Onset 30 min, peak 2–4h, duration 5–8h
- Intermediate-acting (NPH/Insulatard): Once or twice daily
- Long-acting (Glargine/Lantus, Detemir): Once daily, no peak, provides basal coverage
- Hypoglycemia management: Glucose gel, orange juice, sugary drink; if unconscious — glucagon injection or IV dextrose 50%

════════════════════════════════════════
RESPIRATORY MEDICATIONS
════════════════════════════════════════

SALBUTAMOL (Albuterol) — Brand: Ventolin, Salamol
- Class: Short-Acting Beta-2 Agonist (SABA)
- Uses: Asthma (rescue inhaler), COPD (reliever), hyperkalaemia (high dose nebulised)
- Dose: 100–200mcg (1–2 puffs) as needed; up to 10 puffs in severe acute asthma
- Side effects: Tremor, palpitations, tachycardia, hypokalaemia (high doses)
- Note: Use with a spacer device for better delivery; if using more than 3 puffs/week → step up preventer therapy

BECLOMETHASONE / FLUTICASONE (Inhaled Corticosteroids — ICS)
- Uses: Persistent asthma — anti-inflammatory preventer therapy; reduces exacerbations
- Dose: Beclomethasone 200–800mcg/day in divided doses; Fluticasone 100–500mcg BD
- Side effects: Oral candidiasis (thrush) — rinse mouth and gargle after use; dysphonia (hoarse voice); systemic effects at high doses (adrenal suppression, osteoporosis)

MONTELUKAST — Brand: Singulair
- Class: Leukotriene Receptor Antagonist
- Uses: Asthma (adjunct to ICS), allergic rhinitis, aspirin-exacerbated respiratory disease
- Dose: 10mg once daily at night (adult)
- Side effects: Headache, GI upset; Neuropsychiatric effects (depression, suicidal ideation) — FDA black box warning; monitor mental health

OMEPRAZOLE / PANTOPRAZOLE (Proton Pump Inhibitors — PPIs)
- Uses: GERD, peptic ulcer disease, H. pylori eradication, NSAID-induced ulcer prevention
- Dose: Omeprazole 20–40mg once daily before food
- Side effects: Headache, GI; Long-term: magnesium deficiency, C. difficile risk, potential vitamin B12 deficiency, osteoporosis (prolonged use)
- Note: Take 30 minutes before first meal for optimal efficacy

════════════════════════════════════════
COMMON OTC MEDICATIONS IN PAKISTAN
════════════════════════════════════════

ANTIHISTAMINES:
- Cetirizine (Zirtec) 10mg once daily — non-sedating, for allergies, urticaria
- Loratadine (Claritine) 10mg once daily — non-sedating
- Chlorphenamine (Piriton) 4mg every 4–6 hours — sedating, useful at night
- Use for: Allergic rhinitis, urticaria, insect bites, allergic conjunctivitis

ANTACIDS / GI:
- ORS (Oral Rehydration Salts) — essential for diarrhea/vomiting: 1 sachet in 200mL clean water
- Omeprazole 20mg — acid reflux
- Antacid (Gaviscon, Maalox) — liquid antacid for immediate relief of heartburn
- Domperidone (Motilium) 10mg before meals — nausea, gastroparesis
- Ondansetron (Zofran) 4–8mg — powerful anti-emetic (requires prescription)

TOPICAL:
- Clotrimazole cream (Canesten) — antifungal for ringworm, athlete's foot, candidal infections
- Hydrocortisone 1% cream — mild inflammatory skin conditions
- Mupirocin (Bactroban) ointment — topical antibiotic for superficial skin infections
- Zinc oxide cream — barrier cream for rash/eczema

VITAMINS/MINERALS:
- Vitamin C 500mg–1g daily — immune support, collagen synthesis
- Vitamin D3 1000–2000 IU daily — bone health, immune function; deficiency extremely common in Pakistan
- Folic acid 400–800mcg daily — essential before and during pregnancy (prevents neural tube defects); 5mg for high-risk
- Ferrous sulphate 200mg 2–3 times daily — iron deficiency anemia (with vitamin C to enhance absorption)
- Zinc 20mg daily — immune support, wound healing, diarrhea treatment in children
